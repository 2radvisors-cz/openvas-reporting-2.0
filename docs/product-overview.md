# Product Overview

## From scan output to accountable remediation

Modern OpenVAS Reporting 2.0 provides the process layer that organisations need after vulnerability scanning. It correlates OpenVAS findings, enriches them with current threat information and guides them through assessment, decision, remediation, verification and closure.

## Typical users

- security coordinators and cybersecurity teams;
- asset owners and IT managers;
- remediation teams and technical specialists;
- risk owners and approvers;
- management and governance roles;
- internal and external auditors;
- platform administrators.

Each role receives an appropriate view and only the actions required for its part of the workflow.

## Prioritisation model

The platform preserves the original scanner severity and adds a configurable risk score. Inputs may include CVSS, EPSS, known exploitation, public exploit availability and the context of the scanned source. This helps organisations distinguish urgent exposure from findings that can be handled through normal planning.

## Governance and evidence

Decisions, assignments, remediation activity, verification and risk acceptance remain traceable. Management reports and audit views provide evidence of how vulnerabilities are being governed rather than only listing technical scan results.

## Customer-controlled deployment

The platform is intended to operate in infrastructure controlled by the customer. Integration and deployment architecture are adapted to the customer's OpenVAS environment, security requirements, network segmentation and operating processes.
