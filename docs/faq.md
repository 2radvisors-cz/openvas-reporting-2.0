# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is OpenVAS Reporting 2.0?

OpenVAS Reporting 2.0 is a commercial vulnerability management and remediation platform developed by 2R Advisors. It imports OpenVAS results, enriches findings with threat intelligence and supports the process from assessment through remediation or controlled risk acceptance.

## Does it replace OpenVAS or Greenbone Vulnerability Management?

No. OpenVAS remains the vulnerability scanner. OpenVAS Reporting 2.0 consumes completed scan results and adds prioritisation, responsibility, workflow, reporting and audit evidence.

## How are vulnerabilities prioritised?

The platform preserves the original OpenVAS severity and can combine CVSS, EPSS, CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities, public exploit intelligence and deployment context such as internet exposure, network type and operating environment.

## Can it manage remediation work?

Yes. Findings can be assessed, assigned to responsible teams, submitted for approval, remediated, verified and closed. Corrective tasks, deadlines, notifications and audit history support the process.

## Does it support risk acceptance?

Yes. Risk acceptance records the decision, justification, compensating controls, responsible owner, validity and review date. Accepted risks remain available in a dedicated register.

## Can access be limited by role and responsibility?

Yes. Role-based access and team responsibility scopes can separate administration, security assessment, asset ownership, remediation, management and audit activities.

## Where is the platform deployed?

It is designed for deployment in infrastructure controlled by the customer. The final architecture and integration are adapted to the customer's OpenVAS environment and security requirements.

## Is the source code available in this repository?

No. This is a public product information repository. It contains no source code, binaries or deployment packages.
